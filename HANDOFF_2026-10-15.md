# Handoff — 2026-10-15 bench session (Derek)

Read this before working on the table. Summary of what changed in the firmware, what the bench
tests found, and what's still broken.

## Correction: no encoder wiring fix needed

The commit message of `973ecad` (and the first version of this note) said the encoders ran from a
~5 V pack and needed level shifting. **That was wrong.** The encoder Vcc (blue) wires are on the
Teensy's 3.3 V pin (confirmed 2026-10-15), so encoder A/B outputs swing 0–3.3 V and Teensy pins
2–9 are safe as wired. The 4.96 V measurement was the motor drivers' VCC (the 5 V logic rail),
which is in spec. One caveat stands: 3.3 V
is just below Pololu's 3.5 V minimum encoder supply, so if counts are ever missed or noisy at
speed, suspect that first.

## Firmware changes in this commit

All in `Astro 331 Lab 8/` (PlatformIO project). Build-verified and running on the table.

1. **Gear ratio corrected: the motors are 6.3:1, not 10:1.** Every gearbox is labeled `GB37-6.3`.
   Confirmed by turning each wheel 10 turns by hand and reading `COUNTS`:
   A 398, B 400, C 409, D 401 counts/turn (expected 64 × 6.25 = 400).
   - `kEncoderCountsPerRev` 640 → **400** (`control_params.h`).
   - **All wheel speeds logged before this fix read 1.6× low**, including everything in
     `test_runs/` from today. Multiply those rpm values by 1.6.
2. **Free speed measured at 12 V:** B and D hold a true ~50 rad/s at 0.30–0.31 duty, so
   **~160–167 rad/s per unit duty** (≈ 1600 rpm free, matching a 6.3:1 37D). `kWheelFreeSpeedRadS`
   100 → 160, and Owen's Manual-mode `kManualFreeSpeedRadS` 88 → 160 (the 88 was in the old
   mis-scaled units). This closes the "UNRESOLVED free speed" note.
3. **Speed-loop gains rescaled** by 100/160 (Kp 0.009 → 0.0056, Ki 0.045 → 0.028) and
   `kWheelCmdLeadRadS` 8 → 13, so loop behavior matches today's tests in the corrected units.
   Speed limits unchanged: command 60 rad/s (~573 rpm), overspeed disarm 70 rad/s.
4. **12 V motor settings** (made earlier, first committed here): back-EMF current limit in
   `WheelMotor::setDuty` (no current sensing; keeps |duty − speed/free_speed| ≤ 2.5 A / 5.5 A
   stall), max duty 0.95, per-12 V-pack comments. Merged cleanly with Owen's Manual-mode commits.
5. **Duty telemetry.** Applied motor duty (after current limit, cap and slew; same sign as speed):
   - USB CSV: `dutyA..dutyD` appended **after** `state` (existing columns keep their positions).
   - SD log + XBee rows: `motor_a_duty..motor_d_duty` appended at the end (XBee ≈ 590–685 B/s
     of the 960 B/s at 9600 baud).
   - **Parsers that expect an exact column count need updating.**
6. **New commands `COUNTS` and `ZERO`** (XBee or USB, any state). `ZERO` zeroes a readout offset
   (doesn't disturb speed/control); `COUNTS` prints raw counts per wheel and wheel turns.
   Use for gear-ratio / encoder checks: `ZERO`, turn a wheel N times by hand (its pack off), `COUNTS`.
7. **Bench scripts added to `tools/`:** `speed_sweep.py` (smooth 0→PEAK→0 sweep) and
   `wheel_hold.py` (hold one wheel or ALL at an rpm, print speed + duty). Both refuse to start
   unless the board is Idle and always send `KILL` on exit.
8. `CLAUDE.md` and header comments updated for all of the above.

## What the bench tests found (data in `test_runs/`, old 1.6× scaling)

- **B and D are healthy:** track speed within ±1 rpm at ~0.30 duty.
- **Wheel A:** needs ~0.42 duty (vs 0.30). Audible **rubbing**, and **pack A is ~1 V low**. The low
  pack explains only ~0.03 of the extra duty; the rest is the rub. *To do:* find what A rubs on
  (wheel vs housing, a wire, mount alignment) and charge pack A.
- **Wheel C — unexplained:** needs a much higher average duty (swinging 0.32–0.69) and its speed
  oscillates (±30–90 rpm, ~1.5–3 s cycle) whether alone or with the others. No audible drag;
  gearing and encoder count are fine at hand speed. The oscillation is the speed loop leaning on
  its slow integrator to cover C's extra load. Remaining suspects: pack C sagging under load,
  C's encoder misbehaving at speed (its 3.3 V supply is below Pololu's 3.5 V minimum), or a
  weak motor C.
  *Next test:* open-loop `TEST C 0.3` vs `TEST B 0.3`, measuring VIN at driver C during the run.
- **Driver swap A↔B** (2026-10-15): the oscillation stayed with wheel A, so the drivers are fine.
  Note A and B's drivers are currently swapped relative to the original build.
- **Start-up stiction:** wheels don't move until the command reaches ~27–29 (old-scale) rpm, then
  jump; low-speed duty from feedforward + PI is below breakaway. A minimum duty when the command
  is non-zero would fix this (not implemented).

## Other notes

- **IMU can hang after a firmware upload.** The BNO085's RST pin isn't connected, so the Teensy's
  reboot doesn't reset it. If the board sends nothing after an upload, power-cycle (logic off,
  unplug USB, wait, replug, logic on). Fix later: wire BNO085 RST to a free pin, or retry IMU init.
- **Yaw drift:** the yaw estimate drifted ~11–18° over tens of minutes idle (gyro-only yaw, by
  design). A proper stationary drift measurement is still to do.
