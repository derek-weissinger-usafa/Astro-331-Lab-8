2 What could have killed it: cause checks

A 3.3 V rail usually fails short after it sees more than its rated voltage. On this table the only higher voltages are the 5 V logic rail and the 9–12.6 V motor packs, so every path from those to the logic side was checked with all power off:

PathWhy it was suspectedResult
Driver VIN → VCC, all four TB9051FTGsThe original orange VIN–VCC jumper would put 12 V on the 5 V rail and Teensy VINOpen
Driver VM → VCC, all fourVM is the protected motor supply (also 12 V)Open
Motor red/black → encoder blue/yellow/white, every motorMotor terminals pass through the encoder board next to its 3.3 V pads; a bridge puts 12 V on the encoder supply and Teensy pins 2–9 (wheel D's encoder failed around the same time)Open
Boost outputAn over-voltage 5 V rail5 V (5.09 V measured earlier)
Cause not found. No wiring path is shorted now, so the damage may have come from a transient: a loose wire touching something while the table moved, a connector plugged in live, or static. The session turned up three loose connections (the IMU's STEMMA/RST wiring and, twice, wheel D's encoder lead), which makes intermittent contact a plausible suspect. Watch the spare's temperature and 3.3 V after any wiring change.

Wheel D's encoder connection is intermittent. It was found almost pulled out and reseated (D then ran normally), failed again about half an hour later (no counts at 0.2–0.25 duty; the firmware's encoder-fault check took D out of service), and works again after a second reseat. If it drops out mid-run, the firmware disables D, which would look like a wheel failure nobody injected.

Open items

  1. Make wheel D's encoder connection permanent: solder or crimp it and add strain relief. Then wiggle-test it while COUNTS is polled; counts should not jump or drop out.
  2. Strain-relieve the IMU's STEMMA, INT and RST wiring, which caused the BNO085 resets on the first power-up.
  3. Confirm the SD card when the next test opens a log.
  4. Keep the dead Teensy out of service (label it). The cause of its failure is unknown, so check the spare's temperature and 3.3 V after wiring changes.
  5. With all four wheels running, FAULT TEST (requirement 3) and SLEW TEST can be tried on the table once D's connection is fixed.
