Driver swap A ↔ B: does the oscillation follow the driver?

2026-10-15. Two identical sweeps (0 → 300 → 0 rpm, all four wheels, SPEED ALL every 0.1 s). Run 1: original wiring. Run 2: the TB9051FTG drivers for A and B swapped, with the Teensy pins still going to the same motors. Speeds are the firmware's 20 Hz USB telemetry (low-passed, τ = 0.1 s).

What the data shows

  • The oscillation stayed with wheel A, so the driver isn't the cause. On A's original driver, A oscillated (hold SD 6.1 rpm) and B was steady (0.5 rpm). After the swap A still oscillates, and slightly more (hold SD 9.1 rpm, peak-to-peak 35 rpm, ramps + hold SD 10.6 rpm). B is still steady (0.6 rpm) on A's old driver. The cause is on wheel A's side: the motor, gearbox or wheel mechanics, its encoder signal, or its pack and wiring if the packs didn't move with the drivers.
  • New problem: in run 2, wheel C was limited to about 100 rpm. From 6 s to 18 s it stays at 92–100 rpm while the command rises to 300, then follows the command again once the command drops below that. In run 1 it tracked normally. All four wheels run the same firmware, so this is a hardware change, most likely from the rewiring: a low or poorly connected pack C, a loose VIN or ground connection on driver C, or something rubbing wheel C.
  • B and D are unchanged between runs. Measurement noise is about ±1 rpm during the hold and about 2 rpm SD on the ramps.
