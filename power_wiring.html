Motor and logic power wiring

Four 3S Epoch packs (one per wheel), one PKCELL pack for the electronics through a Pololu U3V70F5 5 V boost, a single-pole motor switch on the motor packs' combined negative, and one star ground point.

Before plugging in any pack
  1. On every driver, remove the short orange wire that ties VIN to VCC. VIN gets only the pack +, VCC gets only the 5 V rail. Left tied, 12 V reaches the Teensy.
  2. Disconnect the drivers from the Teensy's VIN pin. The Teensy VIN is fed only by the U3V70F5 now.
  3. Cut the VIN–VUSB trace on the bottom of the Teensy.

System overview

Pack + (9–12.6 V) 5 V rail Ground / pack − PWM signals ● = connection · lines crossing without a dot do not connect

The orange bundle stands for each driver's own two PWM wires (orange and blue) from the Teensy; they are not shared. The pack + leads never touch each other; only the negatives are joined, and only through the motor switch.

Wire all four TB9051FTG carriers the same way. Wheel letter X is A, B, C or D; the table below gives each wheel's Teensy pins.

Rules that keep it safe

  • Each pack + goes to one driver's VIN and nothing else. Never join two pack + leads.
  • Pack negatives reach ground only through the motor switch.
  • Every ground (four driver GNDs, motor switch, Teensy, PKCELL −/boost GND) gets its own wire to the star point.
  • Add about 33 µF electrolytic across the U3V70F5's VIN and GND.
  • Put a plug on each pack lead so packs come out for charging.

Power-up order

  1. Logic switch on. Wait a few seconds: firmware zeroes the PWM pins up to 3 s after boot.
  2. Motor switch on.
  3. To shut down: motor switch off, then logic switch off.
